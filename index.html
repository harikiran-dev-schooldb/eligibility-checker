📌 Admission Process:

  • 🎓 Either parent (Father or Mother) must possess a minimum Graduation qualification.
  • 🏫 The student must be transferring from a Government / CBSE / ICSE / Recognized school.
  • 💵 Application Fee: ₹500 (non-refundable).
  • 📝 Entrance examination is mandatory for admissions from UKG onwards.
  • ✅ Upon qualifying the entrance test, an Admission Fee of ₹12,000 is payable.
  • 📈 Annual school fees are subject to an increase of 8%–10% every academic year.
  • 👕 Books and uniform charges are separate and not included in the school fees.

📄 Documents to be Submitted:

  • 📸 Three (3) recent passport-size photographs of the student.
  • 🧾 Copy of the student’s Birth Certificate.
  • 🏫 Original Transfer Certificate from the previous school (mandatory from UKG onwards).
  • 📘 Copy of the Report Card of the last attended class.
  • 🏠 Residence Proof of either parent (Rent Agreement / Voter ID / Electricity Bill, etc.).
  • 🪪 Aadhaar Card copies of the Student, Father, and Mother.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Three (3) passport-size photographs of Father and Mother.
  • 📜 Caste Certificate of either the Student or the Father (where applicable).

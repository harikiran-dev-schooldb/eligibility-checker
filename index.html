📌 Admission Process

  • At least one parent should be a graduate
  • Students transferring must be from a recognised school
  • Application fee of ₹500 (non-refundable) is applicable
  • Entrance assessment is mandatory from UKG onwards
  • Admission fee of ₹12,000 is payable upon selection
  • Annual fee revision may range between 8% and 10%
  • Cost of books and uniforms is charged separately

📄 Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Transfer Certificate (UKG and above)
  • Previous academic year Report Card
  • Aadhaar copies of student and parents
  • Residential address proof
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Caste Certificate, if applicable

📌 Admission Process

  • 🎓 At least one parent must hold a minimum Graduate-level qualification.
  • 🏫 The student must be transferring from a recognised school.
  • 💵 Application Fee: ₹500 (non-refundable).
  • 📝 Entrance examination is mandatory from UKG onwards.
  • ✅ Admission Fee of ₹12,000 is applicable upon qualifying in the entrance test.
  • 📈 Annual tuition fees are revised every year by approximately 8%–10%.
  • 👕 Charges for books and uniforms are separate.

📄 Documents to be Submitted

  • 📸 Three recent passport-size photographs of the student.
  • 🧾 Copy of the Birth Certificate.
  • 🏫 Original Transfer Certificate (mandatory from UKG onwards).
  • 📘 Report Card of the last attended class.
  • 🏠 Residential proof of the parent/guardian.
  • 🪪 Aadhaar Card of the student and both parents.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Passport-size photographs of Father and Mother.
  • 📜 Caste Certificate (if applicable).
